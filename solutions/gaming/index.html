The problem

What the card rail costs you here

Card declines by country

Issuers block gaming merchant codes wholesale in many markets. A deposit that fails is a player who goes elsewhere, and the decline rate never shows up as a line item.

Chargebacks as a strategy

Deposit, play, lose, dispute. High risk acquirers price this in with a rolling reserve of five to ten percent and a dispute fee on top.

Withdrawals are slow and expensive

A bank wire back to a player in another country costs real money and takes days. Every day it takes is a support ticket.
