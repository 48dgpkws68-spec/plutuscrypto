Invoices

B2B trade settled before the weekend

An invoice to a customer in another country is paid by wire, arrives short of fees, lands days later and has to be matched by hand. A payment link on the invoice does all of that better.

1 linkper invoice, with the reference baked in
15 minrate lock
1 dayto your bank
Nonereconciliation by hand

The problem

What the card rail costs you here

Wires take days and lose money

An international wire arrives short of intermediary fees, days after it was sent, with a reference that has been truncated on the way.

Reconciliation is manual

Matching what arrived to what was invoiced is somebody's afternoon, every week.

Payment terms are a loan

Net 30 on a cross border invoice is a month of financing the customer for free, plus the days the wire takes on top.

How Plutus fits

What we switch on for you

The product is the same underneath. These are the pieces that matter in b2b trade, in the order most merchants adopt them.

Payment links and invoicesRecurring billingDashboardAPI
1

Invoice with a link and a QR

Generate the invoice in the dashboard or from your accounting system through the API. The link carries the invoice number, so the payment matches itself.

2

Paid in full, every time

The customer pays the quoted amount in the asset they hold. No intermediary skims a fee, and a shortfall is flagged instead of silently landing short.

3

Recurring for retainers

Monthly invoices to the same customer go out on schedule and remind themselves.

4

Export to your books

Settlements match your bank line for line and export to CSV or your bookkeeping package.
