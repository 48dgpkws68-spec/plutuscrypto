The problem

What the card rail costs you here

Card fees stack up

A percentage plus a fixed fee plus a cross border surcharge plus a dispute fee. On a €600 order the true cost of a card is rarely under two and a half percent, and you find out at the end of the month.

Chargebacks arrive late

Friendly fraud lands 60 to 120 days after delivery, when the goods are gone and the evidence is thin. High ticket categories lose a percent or more of revenue this way.

Crypto buyers leave

A meaningful slice of online spend now sits in wallets. A shop with no crypto option sends those buyers to a competitor that has one, and never sees the lost sale in any report.
