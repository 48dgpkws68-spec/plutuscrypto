Hosted checkout

A payment page that looks like yours

Redirect the customer to a page carrying your name, your logo and your colours. They pick an asset, scan a code, and come back to your order confirmation. You host nothing and maintain nothing.

  • Your brand on every screen, including the receipt
  • Thirteen assets across eight networks, or a shortlist you choose
  • Rate locked for fifteen minutes from the moment the page opens
  • Return URLs for success, cancel and expiry, plus a signed webhook
  • Twelve languages, currency shown in your settlement currency

Drop in widget

Stay on your own page

One script tag adds a crypto option to the checkout you already have. The widget opens inline, handles asset choice, the QR and the countdown, and hands you back a payment id when it is done. Your customer never leaves.

  • Inline or modal, themed from six variables
  • Works alongside your card and wallet buttons
  • Fires DOM events you can hook into for analytics
  • Falls back to the hosted page on very old browsers

Payment links and invoices

No integration, just a link

Create a link in the dashboard with an amount, a reference and an expiry, and send it however you like: mail, chat, a QR on a printed invoice. Reusable links for a fixed price product, single use links for one order.

  • Optional fields for the customer to fill in, like an order number
  • Expires after a time, a number of uses, or when paid once
  • Invoice mode attaches a PDF with the link and the QR
  • Every link reports to the same dashboard and the same webhooks

In store terminal

At the counter, in the showroom, on the road

Type the amount, turn the screen to the customer, they scan, you see it land. Runs as a web app on any phone or tablet, or on a dedicated terminal with a printer. Each till has its own reference so the ledger tells them apart.

  • Customer display with a big amount and a big QR
  • Release on first sight for small tickets, wait for finality on big ones
  • Printed or emailed receipt with the transaction hash
  • Works on a poor connection; the network does the confirming

Payouts and refunds

Money out, as easily as money in

Refund a customer to the address they paid from with one click. Pay a supplier, a contractor or an affiliate in the asset they prefer. Upload a file of five hundred addresses and let the batch run while you do something else.

  • Refund all or part of any confirmed payment
  • Payouts from a stablecoin float you set aside, topped up at settlement
  • Batches with a review step and a second approver if you want one
  • Every payout carries its transaction hash back to your ledger

On ramp and off ramp

Both directions, one balance

By default everything you receive is converted and sent to your bank. If you would rather keep a stablecoin balance for payouts, refunds or supplier bills, turn the tap: choose how much to hold, and we top it up or drain it at every settlement.

  • Buy stablecoin from your fiat balance at the quoted rate
  • Sell any supported asset into euros, pounds or dollars
  • Set a target float and let settlement keep it there
  • Everything on one statement, in the currency you report in

Recurring billing

Same customer, every month

Crypto has no card on file, so recurring billing works by invitation: we send the customer a fresh payment request on your schedule, remind them if it goes unpaid, and tell you the moment it is settled. Good for subscriptions, memberships and retainers.

  • Schedules weekly, monthly, quarterly or on your own dates
  • Reminder cadence you set, with a grace period before you are told
  • Customers can pay early or pay several periods at once
  • Mark a subscription paused or cancelled without losing history
