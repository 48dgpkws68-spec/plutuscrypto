What we accept

Thirteen assets, eight networks

The coins your customers actually hold, on the networks they actually use. Every one of them converts to euros, pounds or dollars at a locked rate and settles to your bank the next business day. You can enable all of them or a shortlist.

Times are typical, not guaranteed. Network congestion can stretch them; the quote lock does not expire while a payment is confirming.

Stablecoins first

USDT on Tron and USDC on Solana are the cheapest and fastest ways to move a euro's worth of value on chain, and they are what most crypto native buyers reach for. If you enable only two things, enable these.

Network fees are ours

Whatever the chain charges to move the coin is inside the three percent, including the days Ethereum is expensive. Your customer sees one amount and you see one fee.

Adding an asset

We add networks when there is real buyer demand and a clean way to screen them. Ask for one and we will tell you honestly whether it is on the list and when.

Bitcoin, step by step

How to accept Bitcoin payments

To accept Bitcoin as a business, open a merchant account with a crypto payment gateway, pick how customers pay (payment link, hosted checkout, shop plugin or in store terminal) and choose the currency you want in the bank. The customer scans a QR code, the rate is locked, and the gateway converts and settles the money to you.

With Plutus Crypto

1

Tell us what you sell

Use the contact form or write to info@plutuscrypto.com. We reply within one business day with the account structure, the rate and the integration route that fits.

2

Business verification

We verify who owns the business, what it sells, where it sells and the bank account we settle to. It takes a few days, mostly waiting on documents.

3

Pick a route

A payment link works the same day with no integration. The hosted checkout is one API call and a redirect. WooCommerce, Magento 2, PrestaShop, OpenCart, Drupal Commerce and Shopware shops install a plugin and paste two keys. At the counter, use the in store terminal.

4

Test in the sandbox

Sandbox keys arrive the same day and pay with free test coins. The live demo shows the same checkout your customers will see.

5

Take the first payment

The customer scans the QR code from any wallet or exchange. The rate is locked for 15 minutes and stays locked while the payment confirms. After two Bitcoin confirmations, typically about 20 minutes, you receive the payment.confirmed webhook and can release the goods.

6

Get paid in your currency

Confirmed payments are bundled into one bank payout in euros, pounds or dollars on the next business day, by SEPA or Faster Payments. Three percent per transaction covers conversion, network fees and settlement.

Do I need a Bitcoin wallet to accept Bitcoin?

No. The default is to convert at the moment of payment and pay you in euros, pounds or dollars. Holding a stablecoin balance is optional, and most merchants only do it to make payouts or refunds in kind.

How long does a Bitcoin payment take?

The payment shows as detected as soon as the network sees it. We mark it confirmed after two confirmations, typically about 20 minutes, and the money reaches your bank the next business day.

What does it cost to accept Bitcoin?

Three percent per transaction, and that is the whole bill: conversion, network fees and settlement to your bank are inside it. No monthly fee, no setup fee and no chargeback fee. See pricing.
