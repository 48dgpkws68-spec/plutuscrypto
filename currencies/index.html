What we accept

Thirteen assets, eight networks

The coins your customers actually hold, on the networks they actually use. Every one of them converts to euros, pounds or dollars at a locked rate and settles to your bank the next business day. You can enable all of them or a shortlist.

13assets
8networks
3settlement currencies
15 minrate lock on every quote

Times are typical, not guaranteed. Network congestion can stretch them; the quote lock does not expire while a payment is confirming.

Stablecoins first

USDT on Tron and USDC on Solana are the cheapest and fastest ways to move a euro's worth of value on chain, and they are what most crypto native buyers reach for. If you enable only two things, enable these.

Network fees are ours

Whatever the chain charges to move the coin is inside the three percent, including the days Ethereum is expensive. Your customer sees one amount and you see one fee.

Adding an asset

We add networks when there is real buyer demand and a clean way to screen them. Ask for one and we will tell you honestly whether it is on the list and when.
